Features

Everything your visitors ask, answered from your pages.

Smart website crawling

Discovers pages through sitemaps and internal links, skips logins and files, and renders JavaScript-heavy pages when needed.

Source-grounded answers

Answers come only from the website's content. Every sentence cites the page it came from, one click away.

AI-powered conversations

Turns static pages into a conversation that remembers context, so follow-up questions just work.

Voice-ready responses

Listen to any answer read aloud, or ask by voice. Transcripts learn the site's own names and terms.

How it works

From URL to assistant in three steps.

  1. 01

    Enter your website

    Paste a website URL and choose how many pages SiteSense may read.

  2. 02

    Let SiteSense analyze it

    It discovers pages, extracts their text, and builds a searchable knowledge base. You can watch every stage live.

  3. 03

    Start intelligent conversations

    Ask anything. The assistant answers from the website's content and links each claim to its source page.
